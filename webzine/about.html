ABOUT

What does it mean to make a transaction on the internet? How have communal computing spaces impacted culture surrounding technology? These are questions that this webzine seeks to explore. In particular - PISONETs, for Filipino youth, evolved into a third space (a place that was neither home nor school) and “turned internet browsing into a communal activity rather than a solitary one,” reflecting the Filipino tendency for “kapwa” or shared community.
